FOREVER LOVE - MUSIC

The song "I Think They Call This Love" is included in assets/i-think-they-call-this-love.mp3.

Music behavior:
- The song DOES NOT play when the website starts.
- It DOES NOT play on the gift, passkey, flower, or home pages.
- It automatically plays when the user opens Our Memories.
- It automatically plays when the user opens A Love Letter.
- Leaving Memories or the Love Letter stops and resets the song.
- The Play/Pause button is shown only on Memories and Love Letter.
